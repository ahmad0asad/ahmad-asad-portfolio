Table 3 · Visual Interpretation

Satellite RGB Composite Cheat Sheet

Standard three-band red-green-blue (RGB) rendering combinations across major optical satellite families for visual inspection and feature confirmation.

Composite Name Sentinel-2 (R - G - B) Landsat 8/9 (R - G - B) Landsat 5/7 (R - G - B) Visual Appearance & Key Analytical Clues
True Color (Natural) B4 - B3 - B2 B4 - B3 - B2 B3 - B2 - B1 Mimics human vision. Healthy vegetation appears vibrant green; urban centers are grey, white, or slate; water is dark navy or black; bare soils appear beige to terracotta brown.
Color Infrared (CIR / Vegetation) B8 - B4 - B3 B5 - B4 - B3 B4 - B3 - B2 Classic false-color CIR. Healthy photosynthesizing vegetation reflects intensely in NIR and renders as bright crimson red; urban impervious structures appear cyan or light blue; turbid water is pale blue, deep water is dark navy.
SWIR Urban & Built-up B12 - B8A - B4 B7 - B5 - B4 B7 - B4 - B3 Built-up urban areas and sealed infrastructure appear magenta, purple, or lavender. Dense canopy appears brilliant lime green. Agricultural fallow soils appear yellow-orange, providing immediate visual separation from impervious surfaces.
Agriculture & Crop Vigour B8A - B11 - B2 B6 - B5 - B2 B5 - B4 - B1 Highlights crop health and field moisture. Vigorously growing crops appear bright green; harvested or fallow fields appear magenta or pink; bare soil appears brown; urban areas appear dull cyan-grey.
Geology & Soil Moisture B12 - B11 - B2 B7 - B6 - B2 B7 - B5 - B1 Utilizes dual SWIR channels to differentiate carbonate and clay minerals. Moist saturated soils appear dark; dry barren sands and rocky outcrops appear bright orange/yellow; vegetation is muted blue.
Atmospheric Penetration B12 - B11 - B8A B7 - B6 - B5 B7 - B5 - B4 Pierces light haze and smoke plumes. Excellent for tracking active forest fire hot spots and burn scar perimeter delineation (burn scars appear dark burgundy/red; active flames glow bright orange).
Bathymetric / Water Quality B4 - B3 - B1 B4 - B3 - B1 B3 - B2 - B1 Enhances underwater depth gradations, suspended sediment plumes, and coastal reef topography using deep blue/coastal aerosol penetrating channels.
