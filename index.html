Scholarly Contributions

Peer-Reviewed Publications & Prototypes

First-author research articles addressing cross-sensor satellite index formulation, soil sealing quantification, and WebGIS decision support tools.

2026 Lead Author
IJRS · Taylor & Francis

A cross-sensor spectral index for soil sealing detection with improved impervious-bare soil discrimination

Ahmad Asad, Alessandra Capolupo, & Eufemia Tarantino

International Journal of Remote Sensing, DOI: 10.1080/01431161.2026.2715141.

Abstract Summary: Introduces the Sealed Urban Index (SUI = Green - SWIR1), engineered specifically to eliminate spectral confusion between impervious urban surfaces and dry fallow agricultural soil across Sentinel-2 MSI and Landsat 8/9 OLI imagery. Benchmarked across contrasting agro-climatic zones with significant accuracy improvements over traditional indices.

Assessing Soil Sealing Dynamics in the Metropolitan Area of Bari Using Sentinel-2, Sealed Surface Index, and Machine Learning Approaches

Ahmad Asad, Alessandra Capolupo, & Eufemia Tarantino

Computational Science and Its Applications – ICCSA 2025 Workshops, Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS), DOI: 10.1007/978-3-031-64605-8. Springer.

Abstract Summary: Evaluates multi-temporal soil sealing dynamics across the Metropolitan Area of Bari (Puglia, Italy). Combines Sentinel-2 optical bands with sealed surface spectral formulations and Random Forest classification to monitor peri-urban encroachment and surface imperviousness.

Physical Discipline & Daily Protocol

Bio-Academic Lifestyle Blueprint

How disciplined physical habits, circadian alignment, and gut-healthy nutrition sustain long hours of computational modeling and research velocity.

The Physical Engine of Academic Productivity

Deriving mathematical unmixing equations and coding petabyte-scale Earth Engine scripts requires sustained mental focus. Physical conditioning and deliberate nutrition are not distractions from research—they are the foundation that eliminates brain fog, stabilizes energy levels, and maintains high analytical output.

Gut-Healthy Nutrition

Nutrition directly dictates cognitive focus. Grounded in the Mediterranean diet of Puglia combined with probiotic support:

  • Signature Shakshuka: Poached pasture-raised eggs in a rich tomato, bell pepper, and garlic base with extra virgin olive oil.
  • Puglian Whole Foods: Fresh Adriatic fish, lentils, chickpeas, and generous cruciferous vegetables.
  • Probiotic Kefir: Daily fermented kefir to support the gut-brain axis and neuro-metabolic balance.
  • Mindful Moderation: Traditional celebratory dishes like Biryani enjoyed only in strict moderation to prevent glucose spikes.
  • 2L+ Hydration Baseline: Consistent mineralized water intake to prevent mental sluggishness.

4–5 Miles Daily Walking

Active aerobic baseline. Walking 8,000 to 10,000 steps every day along the Adriatic seaside promenade in Bari:

  • Circadian Daylight: Natural morning sunlight exposure that anchors melatonin synthesis and deep nighttime sleep.
  • Screen-Free Problem Solving: Walking breaks allow complex algorithm bugs and paper outlines to organize organically.
  • Low-Impact Recovery: Promotes steady cardiovascular conditioning without causing muscular fatigue before deep work blocks.
