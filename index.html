Scientific Core

Specialized Geospatial & AI Research Pillars

Bridging theoretical satellite radiative transfer and advanced machine learning models to solve pressing environmental and climate challenges.

Satellite Remote Sensing & Radiative Transfer

Multi-sensor optical satellite integration combining Sentinel-2 and Landsat. Formulation of robust spectral indices, pure-pixel extraction techniques, and spectral unmixing models across heterogeneous land-use terrains.

Sentinel-2 Landsat Spectral Unmixing Radiative Transfer

Machine Learning & Surface Classification

Developing supervised and unsupervised machine learning pipelines for high-accuracy land-use/land-cover (LULC) dynamics, multitemporal change detection, and soil sealing quantification across diverse agro-climatic zones.

Random Forest Scikit-Learn LULC Dynamics Time-Series

Cloud Geospatial Computing & Big Data

Architecting automated batch processing workflows with Google Earth Engine (GEE API) and openEO. Scalable analysis on petabyte-scale satellite archives, paired with PostGIS and Python geospatial libraries.

Google Earth Engine openEO Python PostGIS / SQL
